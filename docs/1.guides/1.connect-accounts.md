---
title: Connect your accounts
description: Bring in banks, crypto, investments, manual accounts, and CSV files, and keep them up to date.
---

Money comes into ViteHub Finance from three pages in the sidebar, under **Connect**:

| Page | What you add there |
| --- | --- |
| **Banks** | Bank accounts over open banking, manual accounts, and CSV imports |
| **Crypto** | Kraken, Binance, and Coinbase exchanges. Bitcoin, Ethereum, Polygon, Solana, XRP Ledger, and Nimiq wallets. |
| **Investments** | Interactive Brokers, plus platforms that can't be connected yet |

Each page lists your connections, and other members' connections that feed an account they share. Below them are the ways to add more.

## Banks

Banks connect through Enable Banking over PSD2, Europe's open banking rules. Access is read-only and can't move money. You sign in on your bank's own page, so your password never leaves the bank.

::steps
### Pick your bank

Open **Banks**. Choose your country, then search for your bank or pick it from the list. Some banks show how often you'll need to reconnect, for example "Reconnect every 180 days".

### Approve access at your bank

Click the bank. Its consent page opens. Sign in there and choose the accounts to share.

### Come back to ViteHub Finance

Your bank sends you back to **Banks**, where you see **Bank connected**. The first sync runs right away and asks for up to a year of transactions. Your bank decides how far back it shares.
::

You must finish at the bank while signed in as the person who started. A bank link you don't finish is dropped after about a day.

::note
If the server uses a free Enable Banking app, each account must also be linked in Enable Banking's control panel before you connect it, once per account. Ask whoever runs the server. See [Free Enable Banking apps](/docs/providers/banks#free-enable-banking-apps).
::

More detail, including what each bank shares: [Banks](/docs/providers/banks).

## Crypto

Open **Crypto** and click a tile, or its **Connect** button.

### Exchanges

[Kraken](/docs/providers/kraken), [Binance](/docs/providers/binance), and [Coinbase](/docs/providers/coinbase) connect with a read-only API key. Create one at the exchange with trading and withdrawals off, then paste it:

| Exchange | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| Kraken | **API key** and **Private key** |
| Binance | **API key** and **Secret key** |
| Coinbase | **Key name** and **Private key** |

**Name** is optional. Click **Check and connect**. ViteHub Finance tries the key before it saves anything, and stores it encrypted.

### Wallets

[Bitcoin](/docs/providers/bitcoin), [Ethereum](/docs/providers/ethereum), [Polygon](/docs/providers/polygon), [Solana](/docs/providers/solana), [XRP Ledger](/docs/providers/xrpl), and [Nimiq](/docs/providers/nimiq) wallets connect with their public address. Paste it under **Address**, give it a **Name** if you like, and click **Add wallet**. There are no keys and nothing to sign.

::warning
Only paste a public address. Never paste a seed phrase or a private key.
::

## Investments

[Interactive Brokers](/docs/providers/ibkr) connects with a Flex Web Service token and the ID of an Activity Flex Query. Open **Investments**, click **Connect** on Interactive Brokers, and follow the steps in the dialog. You paste a **Flex token** and a **Query ID**, then click **Check and connect**. ViteHub Finance runs the query once to check it, and saves nothing until that works.

The token can only fetch Flex reports. It can't trade or move money.

[MyInvestor](/docs/providers/myinvestor)'s current account connects as a bank. Its funds, ETFs, and pension plans connect under **Investments**: you add each one with its units, and the app prices them every morning at Morningstar's NAV.

Trade Republic, DEGIRO, and Scalable Capital show as **Coming soon**. Export your transactions from them as a CSV and import it instead.

## Manual accounts

Use a manual account for cash, a pension, a loan, a property, or a bank you can't connect. On **Banks**, under **Manual account**, click **Add**. You can also click **Add account** on the **Accounts** page.

Fill in:

- **Name**
- **Kind**: Checking, Savings, Cash, Credit card, Loan, Investments, Crypto, or Other
- **Currency**
- **Current balance**, optional. Use a negative number for what you owe, like a loan.
- **Bank or provider**, optional
- **Who sees it**: **Shared** or **Private**. This shows when your household has more than one person.

A manual account never syncs. To change its balance, open the account and use **Update balance** in its settings. ViteHub Finance saves each update as that day's balance in the account's history.

## CSV import

Most banks let you export transactions as a CSV file. You can import one into any account you can edit. Open it from **Import** on **Banks**, **Import CSV** on **Accounts**, or **Import a CSV** below the tiles on **Crypto** and **Investments**.

The full walkthrough is in [Import a CSV](/docs/guides/import-a-csv).

## What syncs and how often

Every connection syncs once a day, in the morning. You can also sync one yourself with **Sync now** on its row.

| Source | Balance | Transactions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Banks | Yes, as the bank reports it | Yes |
| Exchanges | Every coin you hold | No |
| Bitcoin, XRP Ledger, and Nimiq wallets | Yes | Yes. They count as transfers, and rewards as income. |
| Ethereum and Solana wallets | Yes | No |
| Interactive Brokers | Positions and cash in each currency | Trades and cash movements |
| Manual accounts | You update it | Only what you import |

Crypto and investments are valued in your household's currency at each day's price. Interactive Brokers positions use IBKR's own prices.

Banks allow only a few syncs a day. After a bank syncs, **Sync now** waits 6 hours before it works again. It tells you the time to try after.

A connection whose sync failed tries again the next morning. Its row shows why it failed.

## Your connections

Each connection is one row on its page. The row shows:

- Its status, such as **Up to date**, **Sync failed**, or **Consent expired**
- How many accounts it feeds. Click to open one.
- When it last synced
- For banks, the date its consent ends

Only the person who made a connection can sync, reconnect, or remove it. The **More** menu on the row has **Sync history**, which lists the last 20 syncs, and **Remove…**. For banks it also has **Reconnect**.

When a connection needs you, its page in the sidebar shows a badge with a count.

## What a tile's status means

Each tile on **Crypto** and **Investments** shows a status:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Available** | Ready to connect. It takes a few minutes. |
| **Connected** | Your household already has one. You can add another account with **Add another**. |
| **Needs setup** | This server can't connect it yet. Whoever runs the server has to set it up. |
| **Coming soon** | It offers no read-only access ViteHub Finance can use. Import a CSV instead. |

Click a tile to see what it reads, what it can't do, and how it signs in.

## Renew an expired bank consent

Your bank gives access for a limited time, at most 180 days. The row on **Banks** shows **Reconnect by** and the date.

- Two weeks before it ends, the date turns into a warning, and the row's button changes from **Sync now** to **Reconnect**.
- About a week before, whoever made the connection gets a reminder email, if your server sends email.
- When it ends, the row shows **Consent expired** and the bank stops syncing.

To renew, click **Reconnect** and approve access at your bank again. ViteHub Finance matches the accounts to the ones you already have, so their history stays.

::tip
Reconnect before the date to avoid a gap in your transactions.
::

Interactive Brokers tokens also expire. Make a new token and connect again with the same Query ID. Your accounts and their history stay.

## Remove a connection

Open the row's **More** menu and choose **Remove…**. Removing stops syncing and deletes the saved credentials. For a bank, ViteHub Finance also asks Enable Banking to end the access.

The dialog asks what to do with the accounts:

- With **Keep the accounts and their history** checked, the default, the accounts stay as manual accounts with all their transactions.
- With it unchecked, ViteHub Finance deletes the accounts and their transactions.

Deleting a single account from its own page doesn't remove its connection. Remove the connection to stop syncing.

## Accounts grouped by connection

One connection can bring in several accounts, for example a bank's current account and its savings pockets.

- On a connection's row, the account count opens a list of its accounts.
- In the sidebar, under **Accounts**, a connection with several accounts folds them under one row named after the bank, with a count. Click it to see those accounts.
- The **Accounts** page groups accounts by kind instead: Cash, Credit and loans, Investments, Crypto, and Other.

New accounts from a connection start shared with the household or private, depending on the household setting **Share new accounts by default**. See [Household and sharing](/docs/guides/household).

::note
Bank connections need the server's operator to set up an Enable Banking app. Without it, **Banks** shows **Bank connections aren’t set up on this server**. Public instances, like the demo, can't connect banks at all and show **Banks aren’t available on this server**. Crypto, Interactive Brokers, manual accounts, and CSV imports work everywhere but the demo, which is read-only.
::
