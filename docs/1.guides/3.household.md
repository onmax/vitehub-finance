---
title: Household and sharing
description: Invite people, choose what each of them sees, and leave or remove someone.
---

A household is the people who see their money together, usually a couple or housemates. When you sign up, you get a household of your own. You start as its only member.

Open **Household** in the sidebar to see its members, invite someone, and change its settings.

## Invite someone

::steps
### Send the invitation

On **Household**, under **Invite someone**, enter their email and click **Send invitation**. They get an email with a link to join.

If your server can't send email, the button is **Create invite link** instead. Copy the link it shows and send it to them yourself.

### They accept

The link opens an invitation page. If they don't have an account yet, they create one there with the invited email. Then they see the household's name and who invited them, and click **Join household**, or **Decline**.
::

An invitation works for 7 days and only once. **Waiting to join** lists the invitations still open. From there you can copy the link again or **Cancel** the invitation. Inviting the same email again cancels the earlier invitation.

If someone opens the link while signed in with a different email, the page asks them to sign in with the invited one.

## Roles

There are two roles. The person who created the household is its **Owner**. Everyone who joins by invitation is a **Member**.

| | Owner | Member |
| --- | --- | --- |
| See shared accounts and add their own | Yes | Yes |
| Invite people and cancel invitations | Yes | No |
| Remove other members | Yes | No |
| Change the household's name, currency, and defaults | Yes | No |
| Leave the household | Not if they're the only owner | Yes |

Roles are about the household. Each account belongs to whoever added it, whatever their role. See [Who can change an account](#who-can-change-an-account).

## Shared and private accounts

Every account is either **Shared** or **Private**.

- **Shared.** Everyone in the household sees it, its balance, and its transactions. It counts in the household's totals.
- **Private.** Only its owner sees it. It stays out of everyone else's lists and totals.

You choose when you add a manual account, under **Who sees it**. To change it later, open the account and use **Who sees it** in its settings. Only the account's owner can change this.

New accounts from a bank or other connection start shared or private depending on the household setting **Share new accounts by default**.

> [!NOTE]
> A private account stays private from everyone, including agents other members connect. An agent you connect sees your own private accounts, because it acts as you.

## Who can change an account

On a shared account, its owner also picks **What others can do**:

- **View.** Others see it but can't change categories, notes, or its name.
- **Edit.** Others can also categorize transactions, add notes, and rename it.

Only the owner changes who sees an account or deletes it, whichever option is set.

## Hide a single transaction

On a shared account, you can hide one of your own transactions from the rest of the household. Open the transaction and turn on **Only me**. Others won't see it in lists, totals, or charts. The account's balance still includes it.

Only the account's owner can do this, from the browser. The switch shows when your household has more than one person.

## Personal and Household views

The menu at the top left of the sidebar picks whose money the app shows. Your choice is remembered on that device.

- **Personal** is the default. It shows only what's yours: your accounts, and on the connect pages, your own banks, investments, and crypto.
- **Household** shows your accounts plus the ones others share. Their private accounts never show, in any view.

Each page's title has a **Personal** or household-name badge, so you can tell which view you're in. Agents and API keys are always your own: they act as you and never see more than you do. Invite people from **Household** in the sidebar.

Budgets and goals can also be personal, so only you see them. See [Budgets and goals](/docs/guides/budgets-and-goals).

## Household settings

The owner changes these at the bottom of **Household**, then clicks **Save**:

- **Name** is the household's name.
- **Currency** is the currency totals and charts use.
- **Share new accounts by default** decides whether accounts anyone connects start visible to the whole household. Each owner can still make theirs private.
- **Suggest categories with AI** shows only when AI categories are available on your server. See [Categories and rules](/docs/guides/categories-and-rules).

Members see these settings but can't change them.

## Leave the household

On **Household**, click **Leave household**. You stop seeing the accounts others share. Your own accounts, connections, and history come with you into a new household of your own.

The household's only owner can't leave.

## Remove a member

As the owner, click the remove button next to their name on **Household**, then confirm. They lose access to the household's shared accounts right away.

Their own accounts, connections, and history go with them into a household of their own. So do their personal budgets and goals.

## What moves when someone leaves

When someone leaves or is removed:

- Their accounts and connections, with every transaction, move to their new household.
- Their personal budgets and goals move with them. Household budgets and goals stay.
- A household goal linked to one of their accounts loses that link.
- Transactions in a category their new household doesn't have, like one you created yourselves, become uncategorized. Their new household's rules then apply to them.

## Delete your account

You can delete your account in **Settings** › **Security**. It deletes your sign-in and your household, with every account, transaction, and connection in it. If others share your household, leave it first so your accounts come with you.

> [!NOTE]
> Whoever runs your ViteHub Finance can limit sign-up to a list of emails. People you invite can still sign up with the invited email. Whether invitations arrive by email also depends on the server.
