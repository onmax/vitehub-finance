Everything in ViteHub Finance works out of the box except bank connections. Banks go through [Enable Banking](https://enablebanking.com), where you register a free app once. Signed in, each step below shows whether it's done.

To have a coding agent walk you through it, copy the prompt into Claude Code, Codex, or another agent opened in your ViteHub Finance checkout. It reads this page and goes step by step.

::setup-prompt
::

## Connect a bank

A free Enable Banking app runs in restricted mode: it reads only the accounts you link in its control panel. So you approve each account twice, once there and once in ViteHub Finance. That's expected; the second approval is the consent the app syncs with.

::steps
### Create an Enable Banking account
:::setup-status{step="account"}
:::

Go to [enablebanking.com/sign-in](https://enablebanking.com/sign-in/), enter your email, and open the sign-in link it sends. Your first sign-in creates the account.

### Register an app
:::setup-status{step="app"}
:::

In the [control panel](https://enablebanking.com/cp/applications), add a new application:

- **Environment**: Production. Sandbox apps only reach Enable Banking's test banks.
- **Private key**: keep the default. Your browser generates the key and downloads it as `<application id>.pem`.
- **Application name**: what banks show on their consent page, such as "ViteHub Finance".
- **Redirect URLs**: ViteHub Finance's address followed by `/api/connect/callback`, such as `https://finance.example.com/api/connect/callback`. Add one for each address you open it from.
- If it asks for a privacy policy and terms, use ViteHub Finance's `/privacy` and `/terms` pages.

### Add the credentials to ViteHub Finance
:::setup-status{step="credentials"}
:::

`ENABLE_BANKING_APP_ID` is the key file's name without `.pem`. `ENABLE_BANKING_PRIVATE_KEY` is the file's contents.

- **`pnpm dev`**: add both to `.env`, the key on one line in double quotes with `\n` between its lines, then restart `pnpm dev`. This prints the key in that form:

  ```bash
  awk '{printf "%s\\n", $0}' key.pem
  ```

- **Cloudflare**: put both as Worker secrets. Each one deploys a new version, so there's nothing to restart.

  ```bash
  npx wrangler secret put ENABLE_BANKING_APP_ID --config .output/server/wrangler.json
  npx wrangler secret put ENABLE_BANKING_PRIVATE_KEY --config .output/server/wrangler.json < key.pem
  ```

- **Node or Docker**: set both in the process's environment and restart it.

A key that starts with `BEGIN RSA PRIVATE KEY` needs converting first: `openssl pkcs8 -topk8 -nocrypt -in key.pem -out key-pkcs8.pem`.

### Link your accounts
:::setup-status{step="link"}
:::

In the control panel, open your app and click **Activate by linking accounts**. Pick your bank, sign in there, and choose the accounts to sync. The first link activates the app. Repeat for every account you want in ViteHub Finance.

### Connect your bank
:::setup-status{step="connect"}
:::

On **Banks**, pick your country and bank, sign in at the bank, and share the accounts you linked. You come back to **Bank connected**, and the first sync brings up to a year of transactions.
::

If something fails along the way, [Banks › Common errors](/docs/providers/banks#common-errors) lists each message and its fix. "The bank shared no accounts" means the account wasn't linked in the control panel first.

## Optional settings

None of these are needed for banks. An agent can set them up too.

| Variable | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `RESEND_API_KEY`, `FINANCE_EMAIL_FROM` | Send invitations, sign-in links, and a reminder a week before a bank's consent ends. Set them when you build. |
| `FINANCE_DOMAIN` or `FINANCE_ORIGIN` | The public address links in emails point at. `FINANCE_DOMAIN` is read when you build for Cloudflare; `FINANCE_ORIGIN` works on any host. |
| `FINANCE_ALLOWED_EMAILS` | Comma-separated emails allowed to sign up. Empty lets anyone sign up; invited people always can. |
| `TYPESAFE_API_KEY` | AI categories for what the rules miss. Each household's owner can add their own key in **Settings › Categories** instead. |
| `FINANCE_CONTACT_EMAIL` | A contact address on the privacy page. |
