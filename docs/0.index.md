ViteHub Finance brings your money into one place and lets you share it with the people you live with.

## What it does

- **Accounts.** Connect European banks, crypto exchanges, crypto wallets, Stripe, and Interactive Brokers. Add cash, a pension, or a loan by hand, or import a bank's CSV export.
- **A shared household.** Invite a partner or housemate. Each account is shared with the household or private to its owner.
- **Categories.** Every transaction gets a category. Rules fill them in as transactions arrive.
- **Budgets and goals.** Set a monthly amount per category and track savings goals.
- **Recurring payments.** ViteHub Finance finds the subscriptions, bills, and income that repeat in your transactions.
- **Forecast.** See where your balance is heading, from your income, bills, and usual spending.
- **Alerts.** Hear when a bank needs reconnecting, a budget runs low, the balance looks low ahead, or a large payment goes out.
- **Agents.** Connect Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, or another MCP client so it can read your money and answer questions about it.

## Get started

::steps
### Create your account

Sign up with your name, email, and a password. You get a household of your own, with a set of categories and rules ready to use.

### Connect your first account

Open **Banks** in the sidebar and pick your bank, or use **Crypto**, **Investments**, or **Payments**. No connection for yours? Add a manual account or import a CSV. See [Connect your accounts](/docs/guides/connect-accounts).

### Invite someone

Open **Household** and use **Invite someone**. They join your household and see the accounts you share. See [Household and sharing](/docs/guides/household).

### Set up your categories

Open **Settings** › **Categories** to rename, add, or remove categories, and **Settings** › **Rules** to sort transactions automatically. See [Categories and rules](/docs/guides/categories-and-rules).
::

> [!NOTE]
> Some features depend on whoever runs your ViteHub Finance. Bank connections need setup on the server. The server can limit sign-up to a list of emails. Invitations and reminders arrive by email only if the server can send it. On the public demo, you can look around but can't change anything: every visitor sees the same household.
