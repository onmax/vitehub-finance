---
title: Banks
description: Connect checking, savings, and card accounts from European banks over PSD2, through Enable Banking.
---

Banks connect through [Enable Banking](https://enablebanking.com), using PSD2 open banking. You sign in at your own bank and approve read-only access. The app never sees your bank password.

::note
**For whoever runs the instance:** bank connections need `ENABLE_BANKING_APP_ID` and `ENABLE_BANKING_PRIVATE_KEY` on the server. Create an app at [Enable Banking](https://enablebanking.com), set both, and restart the server. Enable Banking is free for your own accounts: a free app reaches the accounts you link in its control panel. Until it's set up, the bank list says **Bank connections aren't set up on this server**.
::

## Connect a bank

::steps
### Open Banks
Go to **Banks** in the sidebar. The bank list is under **Connect a bank**.

### Choose your country
Pick your country in the country menu. The list covers EU and EEA countries and the United Kingdom. The app remembers your choice in this browser.

### Find your bank
Banks people use most come first, under **Popular in** your country. Below them is **All banks**. Type in **Search banks…** to find yours. In a long list, click **Show all** at the end to see every bank.

### Go to your bank
Click your bank. The app sends you to your bank's sign-in page.

### Give consent
Sign in at your bank, choose the accounts to share, and approve. Share at least one account.

### Come back
Your bank sends you back to **Banks**. You see **Bank connected**, and the accounts are there. The first sync brings up to a year of transactions.
::

Finish the consent within 30 minutes, signed in as the same person who started it. Otherwise you see an error and need to start again. A link left at the bank's consent page shows **Waiting for the bank**, and the app removes it after a day.

The public demo can't connect banks; it's read-only. Where bank connections aren't set up, import a CSV export from your bank instead. See [Import a CSV](/docs/guides/import-a-csv).

## What it reads

For each account you share, the app reads:

- Its balance. It prefers the available balance when the bank sends several.
- Its booked transactions: date, amount, description, and the payee. Pending transactions aren't read.
- Its IBAN and currency, and its type: checking, savings, card, or loan, as the bank reports it.

## What it can't do

- Move money or make payments. The access is read-only.
- See your bank password. You only sign in at your bank.
- Read accounts you didn't share in the consent.

## Several accounts and currency pockets

One connection is one consent at one bank. It can hold several accounts, and each one becomes its own account in the app. Each currency account the bank shares is its own account too, even when several share one IBAN.

The app names each account by bank and currency, and adds the bank's product name when it sends one, for example `Example Bank EUR`. When two accounts would get the same name, it adds the last four digits of the IBAN, like `Example Bank EUR ···1234`. It never uses the account holder's name.

On **Banks**, a connection with several accounts shows how many it has. Click the count to open one.

## Consent length and renewal

A consent lasts up to 180 days. Some banks allow less. The bank list shows **Reconnect every** and the number of days for those banks.

### Before it ends

Each bank connection shows **Reconnect by** and the date. In the last 14 days, the date turns amber, and the row shows **Reconnect** in place of **Sync now**. **Alerts** also tells you when a bank's access ends within 14 days. If the server can send email, the person who connected the bank gets a reminder a week before.

### After it ends

When the consent runs out, the connection stops syncing:

- Its status says **Consent expired**.
- The row says **Consent ended** and the date.
- Under it: "The bank consent has expired. Reconnect the bank to keep syncing."

Your accounts and their history stay. They just stop updating.

### Reconnect

::steps
### Click Reconnect
On **Banks**, click **Reconnect** on the connection. It's also in the row's **...** menu at any time.

### Approve again at your bank
Sign in and approve, as you did the first time.
::

The accounts match the ones you had, by the bank's account ID or by IBAN and currency, so their history stays. Only the person who connected the bank can reconnect it.

## Sync

Bank connections sync every morning on their own. **Banks** shows **Synced daily** above the list, and each row says when it last synced.

To sync sooner, click **Sync now** on the connection. Banks allow only about 4 unattended fetches a day under PSD2. So **Sync now** works once every 6 hours per bank. Before that, it asks you to try again after a time, in UTC.

The first sync reads up to a year of transactions. Later syncs read from 10 days before the last sync, so late bookings still arrive. A transaction already in the app isn't added twice.

**Sync history** in the row's **...** menu lists the last 20 syncs, how many new transactions each brought, and any error.

## Remove a bank

In the row's **...** menu, click **Remove…**. The app ends the consent at the bank and deletes the saved credentials. **Keep the accounts and their history** is on by default and keeps the accounts as manual accounts. Untick it to delete them and their transactions.

## Common errors

These messages show on **Banks**: at the top after you come back from your bank, under a connection that stopped syncing, or in its **Sync history**.

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The bank consent has expired. Reconnect the bank to keep syncing. | Click **Reconnect**. |
| Your bank allows about 4 syncs a day. Try again in a few hours. | Wait. The next morning's sync also picks it up. |
| Try again after (a time) UTC. Banks allow only a few syncs a day. | **Sync now** ran less than 6 hours ago. Wait until that time. |
| The bank or Enable Banking isn't answering. Try again later. | The bank or Enable Banking is down. The next sync retries. |
| Enable Banking returned an error. Try again later. | Try again later. The message adds the details. |
| Couldn't sync 1 of 3 accounts. | Some accounts synced and some didn't. The message names the ones that failed. It shows in **Sync history**. |
| This bank link expired. Connect the bank again. | You took more than 30 minutes at the bank. Start again. |
| The bank sent back an incomplete link. Connect the bank again. | Start again from the bank list. |
| The bank didn't give access. Try again and approve it at your bank. | You declined or cancelled at the bank. Start again and approve. |
| The bank didn't confirm access. Connect the bank again. | Start again from the bank list. |
| The bank shared no accounts. Try again and select at least one. | Start again and share at least one account. |
| Sign in as the person who started connecting this bank, then try again. | Someone else is signed in. Sign in as the person who clicked the bank. |
| Connected, but the first sync failed. | The connection exists. Click **Sync now** later, or wait for the next morning. |
| Couldn't read this connection's saved credentials. Reconnect it. | Click **Reconnect**. If you run the server, check whether `FINANCE_ENCRYPTION_KEY` changed. |
| Enable Banking rejected the app ID or private key. | For whoever runs the server: check `ENABLE_BANKING_APP_ID` and `ENABLE_BANKING_PRIVATE_KEY`. |

In languages other than English, the app shows a translated summary of the error instead of the exact message.
