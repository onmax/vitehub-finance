title: DEGIRO
description: DEGIRO can't be connected yet. Track it with a manual account and its account statement export.
---

DEGIRO shows as **Coming soon** under **Investments**. It has no API that a third party can read with your permission. Until that changes, track it in two parts.

## Track its value

Add a [manual account](/docs/guides/connect-accounts#manual-accounts) for DEGIRO, with the kind **Investments**. Set its balance to your portfolio's value in DEGIRO, and update it when you want the overview to catch up.

## Bring in its transactions

DEGIRO's website exports two files. The **Account statement** has the cash movements: deposits, withdrawals, dividends, taxes, and fees. **Transactions** has the buys and sells.

::steps
### Export the account statement
On DEGIRO's website, open **Activity** › **Account statement**. Set the start date to when you opened the account and the end date to today, then export it as CSV.

### Import it
In ViteHub Finance, open the manual account and click **Import CSV**. Check the columns as [Import a CSV](/docs/guides/import-a-csv) describes, then import.
::

> [!NOTE]
> DEGIRO's CSV uses Dutch formats whatever your language: dates as `dd-MM-yyyy` and a comma as the decimal separator. Check the **Date** format and the amounts in the preview before you import.

Money you send to DEGIRO from a connected bank shows as a transfer on that bank's account, not as spending.
