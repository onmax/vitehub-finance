---
title: Scalable Capital
description: Scalable Capital can't be connected yet. Track it with a manual account and its CSV transaction export.
---

Scalable Capital shows as **Coming soon** under **Investments**. It has no API that a third party can read with your permission. Until that changes, track it in two parts.

## Track its value

Add a [manual account](/docs/guides/connect-accounts#manual-accounts) for Scalable Capital, with the kind **Investments**. Set its balance to your portfolio's value in Scalable Capital, and update it when you want the overview to catch up.

## Bring in its transactions

Scalable Capital's broker exports your transactions as a CSV from its transaction list, where you can filter them first. Scalable Capital offers the export in its PRIME+ plan; check your plan if you don't see it.

::steps
### Export the CSV
In Scalable Capital's broker, open your transactions, filter them if you like, and export them as CSV.

### Import it
In ViteHub Finance, open the manual account and click **Import CSV**. Check the columns as [Import a CSV](/docs/guides/import-a-csv) describes, then import.
::

Money you send to Scalable Capital from a connected bank shows as a transfer on that bank's account, not as spending.
