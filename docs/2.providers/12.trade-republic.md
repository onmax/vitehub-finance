Trade Republic shows as **Coming soon** under **Investments**. It has no API that a third party can read with your permission. Until that changes, track it in two parts.

## Track its value

Add a [manual account](/docs/guides/connect-accounts#manual-accounts) for Trade Republic, with the kind **Investments**. Set its balance to your portfolio's value in Trade Republic's app, and update it when you want the overview to catch up.

## Bring in its transactions

Trade Republic's app exports every transaction as a CSV: orders, savings plans, dividends, interest, card payments, fees, deposits, and withdrawals.

::steps
### Export the CSV
In Trade Republic's app, tap your profile, then **Account statements** › **Transaction export**. Choose the period, for example from the beginning, and create the file. The export is in the app only, not on the website.

### Import it
In ViteHub Finance, open the manual account and click **Import CSV**. Check the columns as [Import a CSV](/docs/guides/import-a-csv) describes, then import.
::

Money you send to Trade Republic from a connected bank shows as a transfer on that bank's account, not as spending.
