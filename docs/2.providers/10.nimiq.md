ViteHub Finance watches a Nimiq address. Watch-only by address means it reads what anyone can see on the blockchain for that address. It never has a private key, so it can never spend or move your coins.

## Addresses it accepts

- `NQ` followed by two check digits and 32 characters, with or without spaces, in any case

Each wallet watches one address. Nimiq addresses carry check digits, so ViteHub Finance catches a typo before it adds the wallet. Anything else gets "That isn't a valid Nimiq address."

## What it reads

- **Balance.** The NIM at the address, staked NIM included.
- **Transactions.** Yes. Each send and receive becomes a transaction in ViteHub Finance, counted as a transfer. Block and staking rewards count as income, including payments from a known validator. Moving NIM into or out of staking doesn't add a transaction, because the balance already counts staked NIM.
- **Staking.** A **Staking** section on the account's page. See [Staking](#staking).
- **History.** The first sync reads up to the 2,000 most recent transactions. Later syncs read the last ten days.
- **Balance chart.** ViteHub Finance rebuilds it day by day from those transactions, up to a year back.

## Staking

The account's page shows the address's staking, read again at every sync:

- **Balances.** Liquid NIM, and stake that is active (earning), inactive (being unstaked), or retired (released after the validator was retired or penalized). These are already in the balance: nothing on this section adds to it.
- **Validator.** The one the stake is delegated to, and whether it's in the public Nimiq validator directory.
- **Identified rewards.** Rewards paid to the address and rewards added to the stake, per month, less staking fees, with their value at today's price.
- **Principal moved.** NIM moved into and out of staking, as gross totals, and NIM that arrived from other addresses. None of it counts as earnings: moving the same NIM between validators counts each time.

> [!WARNING]
> Identified rewards are a documented subset, not your lifetime earnings. A payment counts as a reward only when it comes from a validator in the public directory, your current validator, or that validator's reward address, so older or custom payout addresses can be missing. Rewards a validator owes you but hasn't paid don't show. Unpaid rewards, investment profit, and APY always read **Unknown**: they need the validator's own accrual data, the full history of what you put in, what the NIM cost, and past prices.

The money values are today's price for those NIM, not what the rewards were worth when they arrived. When a staking read fails or comes back incomplete, the section keeps the last complete read and says when it was taken.

Staking data comes from the NimiqWatch node, NimiqWatch's restake index at `v2.nimiqwatch.com`, and the Nimiq validator directory. History starts at proof-of-stake's launch on 19 November 2024.

## Where the data comes from

- **Source.** The public NimiqWatch node at `rpc.nimiqwatch.com`. No account or key is needed.
- **Value.** NIM at CoinGecko's price, in your household's currency. Each transaction uses the price on its day. Transactions older than a year use the oldest price available.
- **Sync.** Every morning, and when you select **Sync now** on its row.

## Add the wallet

1. Open **Crypto** in the sidebar and select **Connect** on the Nimiq tile.
2. Paste the public address into **Address**.
3. Optionally, give it a **Name**. Without one, it's called Nimiq wallet.
4. Select **Add wallet**.

The server checks the address before it saves it, then reads it right away.

> [!WARNING]
> Only paste the public address. Never paste a seed phrase or private key.

For more on Nimiq, see [nimiq.com](https://www.nimiq.com/).
