---
title: Stripe
description: Import your Stripe payment balance and transaction history with a restricted read-only key.
---

Connect your own Stripe merchant account under **Payments**. ViteHub Finance reads the Payments balance ledger, including payments, refunds, disputes, fees, and payouts. It creates one account per settlement currency.

## Create a restricted key

1. Open [Stripe's API keys](https://dashboard.stripe.com/apikeys) in live mode.
2. Create a **restricted API key**. Give **Accounts** and **Balance** **Read** access. Leave every other permission at **None**.
3. Open **Payments** in ViteHub Finance and select **Connect** on Stripe.
4. Paste the key beginning with `rk_live_`. Optionally, give the connection a name.
5. Select **Check and connect**.

The server checks the account identity, balance, and ledger permissions before saving the key encrypted. Test keys, publishable keys, and unrestricted secret keys are refused. There is no platform OAuth setup or Stripe Connect application to configure.

See [Stripe's restricted-key guide](https://docs.stripe.com/keys/restricted-api-keys). Permission labels can vary in Stripe's dashboard. The connector reports a permission error if its read requests are refused.

## What comes in

- Available and pending funds separately. The account total includes both.
- Balance movements, with gross amount, fee, net amount, settlement status, and availability date.
- Historical movements on the first sync. Later syncs overlap recent history and revisit older pending movements until they settle.
- Daily updates and **Sync now** from the connection row.

Each Stripe balance transaction is imported once. Settlement updates that same transaction and keeps your notes, categories, and review decisions.

The app stores ledger identifiers and amounts. It does not fetch customer profiles, card details, invoice lines, or charge descriptions. Transaction labels describe the kind of ledger movement, so individual customers are not named. It cannot create payments, issue refunds, or move funds with the restricted key described above.

## Fees and totals

Totals use the net movement. A €100 payment with a €3 fee imports as **+€97**. Its details show €100 gross and €3 fees; the fee is not deducted twice. Refunds, disputes, reversals, and standalone fees follow the signed ledger amounts.

This is a cash ledger, not an invoicing or tax report. Gross sales and processing expenses are visible in transaction details, but are not separate categories in spending reports.

## Payouts and bank deposits

Payouts and their reversals, top-ups, reserves, and explicit currency movements are transfers. They are excluded from spending and income. Transfers between Stripe merchants can be business payments, so they are not all automatically treated as transfers between your own accounts.

When the bank account receiving a payout is also connected, the app tries to match opposite movements of the same amount and currency within its matching window. Check the bank deposit's category after syncing. If it was not matched, set it to **Transfer** to avoid counting your Stripe income twice. Different dates, currency conversion, or a bank grouping deposits can prevent an automatic match.

## Balances and forecasts

Stripe accounts appear under **Other**. They count toward net worth, including pending funds, unless you exclude the account in its settings. They are excluded from the cash forecast because pending settlement and payout timing differ from money already in your bank.

The balance chart starts with the first sync. Importing old ledger movements does not reconstruct Stripe's historical available and pending balances.

## Rotate or remove a key

Create another restricted key with the same permissions and connect it again. The app recognizes the same Stripe account for your user and updates its saved key while preserving its accounts and history. Revoke the old key in Stripe after the new key works.

Removing the connection deletes its saved key. You can keep the imported accounts and history as manual accounts, or delete them too. Revoke the key in Stripe if you no longer need it.

This connector reads the merchant's Payments balance. Stripe Treasury, issuing balances, and other merchants connected to a platform are outside its scope. Large histories that exceed the connector's page limit fail the sync rather than importing an incomplete ledger.
